## App issues found (not changed)

1. **GratuityCalculator.tsx** counts a leftover of exactly 6 months as a year (`fraction >= 0.5`); s.53(2) counts only a part "in excess of six months". 7 years 6 months at ₹30,000: app ₹1,38,462, Code ₹1,21,154. The website calls the formula with whole counted years only.
2. **GratuityCalculator.tsx** treats 4 years 240 days as eligible (court reading); the Code says 5 years. The website requires 5 years.
3. **GratuityCalculator.tsx** tax: assumes a ₹20 lakh exempt limit "per Income Tax Act s.10(10)" — 1961 Act reference; under the 2025 Act it is s.19 Table Sl. 5/6 and the notified limit was not verifiable.
4. **schemes.ts c-nps**: eligibility "aged 18–70" (now 18–85); benefits "minimum 40% annuity" (now 20% for non-government; its own `notes` already say so).
5. **EPFCalculator.tsx** always routes 8.33% (capped) to EPS; a member outside EPS (joined above the ceiling) has the full employer 12% in EPF. The website states the EPS-member assumption.
