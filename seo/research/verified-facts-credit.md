## RBI — credit information (CIBIL page)
Source A: RBI (Credit Information Companies) Directions, 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/378, 28 Nov 2025 (updated 1 Jul 2026) —
https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12926
- Para 21(7): one Free Full Credit Report including credit score, once a year (January–December), electronically on request after authentication; link on the CIC's homepage; scores calibrated from 300 to 900.
- Para 4(2): four CICs — CRIF, Equifax, Experian, TransUnion CIBIL.
- Para 37(i): SMS/email alert to customers when their CIR is accessed by specified users.
- Para 41: ₹100 per calendar day if a complaint is not resolved within 30 calendar days; credited within 5 working days of resolution; reasons for rejection; complaints about score computation/model excluded (41(8)(iii)).

Source B: RBI (Commercial Banks – Credit Information Reporting) Directions, 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/156 —
https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13154
- Para 10(2): data as on 9th, 16th, 23rd and last day of the month; full file by 5th of next month; incremental within 4 calendar days.
- Para 10(3): no repayment, including the last instalment, left unreported.
- Para 34(1)(i): SMS/email alert while submitting default / days-past-due information.
- Para 35: 30 days overall; CI 21 calendar days; CIC the remaining 9 days; ₹100/day.
- Para 38(5) (best practices for board-approved policies): first-time borrowers' applications should not be rejected just because they have no credit history.
- The document prescribes no minimum credit score (checked: it does not use the term "credit score").

## App issues found (not changed)
1. **APP BUG** `src/pages/OverdraftCalculator.tsx` (over-limit branch): drawings above the limit are charged `excess × (rate + excessPenaltyRate)` — penal interest added to the rate. RBI RBC Directions para 351 bar penal interest from 1 Apr 2024 (all loans from 30 Jun 2024); breaches attract a separate, non-capitalised penal charge. Example: limit ₹10 lakh, drawn ₹11 lakh, 10.5%, 30 days → app adds ₹1,027 penalty interest (₹1 lakh × 12.5% × 30/365) on top of ₹8,630. Website caps drawings at the limit.
2. `src/utils/loanStatusEngine.ts`, `src/components/LiveLoanStatus.tsx`, `src/pages/LoanDetail.tsx`: fixed-rate foreclosure charge defaults to a hard-coded 3% (unsourced) and labels cite "RBI 2014 circular"; current rule is the 2025 Directions para 353 (charge per the lender's disclosed policy, on the amount prepaid; floating-rate business loans to individuals/MSEs also nil). `en.json liveStatus.rbiNoCharge` says the ban covers "floating rate home loans" only.
3. `src/pages/expenses/CreditCardAdd.tsx`: "Interest-free Period" shows statement-to-due days (+30 when wrapping); RBI defines the period from transaction to due date (para 5(16)), so the app shows only the minimum.
4. `en.json cibil.*`: "Payment history = 35% of score", "1 in 5 reports has errors", "dispute takes 30 days … +100", fixed point impacts (−50 to −100) are unsourced; `cibil.rbiSource` cites "Master Direction … Jan 2025", now replaced by the 28 Nov 2025 Directions. `LOAN_CIBIL_RATES` score-to-rate bands are unsourced (SEO_PLAN §8) — not used on the website.
