## App issues found (not changed — Android app out of scope; see APP_IDEAS.md)

1. POTD calculator compounds interest across years; the scheme pays interest yearly and adds none on unwithdrawn interest.
2. "RBI mandates 18% GST on all credit card finance charges" (en.json ccInterest.gstNote, disclaimer): GST comes from GST law, not RBI.
3. Tax disclaimer and labels say "AY 2027-28"; under the Income-tax Act, 2025 it is "tax year 2026-27".
4. Gold loan default rate ₹13,600/g vs IBJA ₹13,420/g (17 Nov 2026).
5. getMinimumDue comment calls 5% of balance the "RBI standard"; RBI only requires no negative amortisation.
6. Mortgage disclaimer cites "RBI Circular 2014" and "2–4%" fixed-rate charges; current source is the 2025 Directions (paras 352–353); 2–4% is unsourced.
