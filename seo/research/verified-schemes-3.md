## c-mgnrega — VB-G RAM G
- Act No. 36 of 2025 (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)) came into force in all States/UTs on **1 July 2026**. Source: Gazette S.O. 2382(E), 11 May 2026, https://vbgramg.dord.gov.in/vbgramg/WriteReaddata/data/Gazette_Notification_Commencement_Act.pdf
- MGNREGA was repealed from the same date. The guarantee is **125 days** per FY. e-KYC-verified Job Cards stay valid until Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards are issued. Work can be demanded orally, on Form 6 or digitally, and must be given within 15 days. Unemployment allowance is ≥¼ of the wage for the first 30 days and ≥½ after that. Wages are paid weekly or within 15 days of the muster roll closing, with delay compensation of 0.05% a day. Work beyond 5 km pays +10%. Fund sharing is 90:10 for NE/Himalayan states, 60:40 for other states and 100% for UTs without a legislature. States may pause works for up to 60 days (Section 6). Source: MoRD FAQ, 11 May 2026, https://vbgramg.dord.gov.in/vbgramg/WriteReaddata/data/FAQ_English_VB-G_RAM_G_Notification_DayMay11,2026.pdf, and the PIB backgrounder, https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/may/doc2026511867601.pdf
- **Wage rates** were notified by the Centre under Section 10, effective 1 July 2026: Andhra 312, Goa 406, Haryana 409, HP scheduled areas 375, Karnataka 382, Kerala 401, Maharashtra 317, Punjab 360, Sikkim's three high-altitude GPs 450, Tamil Nadu 345, Telangana 308, A&N 348/367, DNH&DD 340, Lakshadweep 348, Puducherry 347, and ₹300 everywhere else. Source: S.O. 3518(E), 30 June 2026, https://vbgramgmatt.dord.gov.in/VBGRAMG/Circulars/2Wage_Rate_notification_for_FY_2026-27_under_VB-GRAMG.pdf
- The national average wage went from ₹298.80 to ₹327.40, and 21 states/regions were raised to ₹300. Source: AIR News, 1 Jul 2026, https://newsonair.gov.in/centre-notifies-revised-wage-rates-under-vb-g-ram-g-act-2025/
- Under Section 5(4), states may provide extra days from their own funds. Source: PIB 4 Aug 2026, PRID 2294301.
- Portal: vbgramg.dord.gov.in. Its menus include "Analytics & Reports" (Dashboard: MIS Report, Payment Dashboard; State Report) and Grievance Redressal. **No helpline number is published**, so none is given on the page.
- Figures computed: 125 × ₹300 = ₹37,500, 125 × ₹409 = ₹51,125, and so on, one per table row.

## c-pmvishwakarma
- Loans: ₹1 lakh over 18 months, then ₹2 lakh over 30 months, at 5% interest with subvention of up to 8%. The 2nd tranche needs the 1st repaid, a standard account, and digital adoption or advanced training, and comes no earlier than 6 months after the 1st. Toolkit: up to ₹15,000 as an e-RUPI/e-voucher after skill assessment. Stipend: ₹500/day. Eligibility: 18+, one per family, no PMEGP/SVANidhi/MUDRA loan in the last 5 years (repaid MUDRA/SVANidhi loans are allowed), government employees excluded. Documents: Aadhaar, mobile, bank details and ration card (or the Aadhaar of all family members). Enrolment is free via CSC or the portal with biometric authentication, followed by GP/ULB, then DIC, then Screening Committee approval. The scheme runs to 2027-28. Source: guidelines v30, https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/cdn/MiscFiles/eng_v30.0_PM_Vishwakarma_Guidelines_final.pdf
- Digital incentive is ₹1 per transaction, capped at 100 a month. Marketing support comes through NCM. The 30 lakh registration target was reached in Sep 2025. Source: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
- Helpline 18002677777 and 17923, email champions@gov.in. The login page asks for mobile + captcha. Source: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/ContactUs

## c-pmsvanidhi
- Cabinet decision of 27 Aug 2025: lending extended to 31 Mar 2030, ₹7,332 crore, 1.15 crore beneficiaries; loans of ₹15k/₹25k/₹50k; RuPay credit card once the 2nd loan is repaid; cashback of up to ₹1,600. Source: https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-restructuring-extension-of-lending-period-beyond-31-12-2024-of-pm-street-vendors-atmanirbhar-nidhi-pm-svanidhi-scheme/
- Loan Operational Guidelines: tenures of 12/18/36 months; minimums of ₹5k/₹15k/₹30k; 7% p.a. interest subsidy credited quarterly while the account is standard; maximum subsidy ₹603/₹1,408/₹5,579. Documents: CoV/ID/LoR, Aadhaar (Voter ID only in Assam/Meghalaya), passbook, and a UPI ID (mandatory). Census-town vendors need a BDO-approved LoR. No credit score, Udyam or project report is needed, there is no prepayment penalty, the 1st and 2nd loans carry no fee, and the loans are CGTMSE-guaranteed with only a DPN. Source: https://www.mohua.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/01/a3a17370145e0870a79df74bfbab766f.pdf
- The status form asks for Application No. + Mobile + OTP. The help email is pmsvanidhi.support@sidbi.in. Source: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/Home/Search and /Home/Contact

## c-nsap — PM Samajik Sahayata Yojana
- Cabinet decision of 14 Oct 2026: ₹53,428 crore for 2026-27 to 2030-31; new names for the scheme and its sub-schemes; disability pension of ₹375 (₹625 at 80+) with a 40% benchmark and eligibility from birth, with guidelines due within 3 months; the other schemes continue as before; 3.20 crore beneficiaries. Source: https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-continuation-of-the-national-social-assistance-programme-for-five-years-from-2026-27-to-2030-31/
- Current amounts: old age ₹200 (60–79) and ₹500 (80+); widow ₹300 (40–79) and ₹500 (80+); disability ₹300 (18–79, severe/multiple) and ₹500 (80+); NFBS ₹20,000 (breadwinner aged 18–59). State top-ups are ₹50–₹5,700, giving an average of about ₹1,100. GPs and municipalities identify beneficiaries, and 94% are paid by DBT. The DLC app launched on 15 Jul 2025. Source: PIB backgrounder, Nov 2025, https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025117686801.pdf

## c-nmms
- 2026-27: applications ran from 1 June to 15 Nov 2026, L1 verification is due 30 Nov and L2 by 15 Dec. There are 1 lakh fresh scholarships a year at ₹12,000 a year, covering only state government, aided and local-body schools. Eligibility: 55% in Class VII (5-point relaxation for SC/ST) and parental income ≤ ₹3.5 lakh. As of 30.10.2026 there were 90,483 fresh and 1,99,531 renewal applications. Source: PIB 31 Oct 2026, https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2330936
- Payment is by DBT through PFMS with two-level verification (PIB 16 Sep 2025, PRID 2167076). The scheme is 100% centrally funded (PIB 7 Mar 2024, PRID 2012402). The extensions to 30 Sep and 31 Oct come from AIR News of 1 Sep and 1 Oct 2026.
- **Left out:** the 60%-in-Class-X renewal rule and the KV/JNV exclusion appear only in secondary or state sources.

## c-pmmvy
- ₹5,000 for the first child in 2 instalments: ₹3,000 after registration plus an ANC within 6 months of the LMP, and ₹2,000 after birth registration plus the first vaccine cycle. ₹6,000 for a second girl child in a single instalment after birth, which requires registration during pregnancy. Eligible categories: SC/ST, 40% disability, BPL, PM-JAY, e-Shram, PM-Kisan women farmers, MGNREGA job card holders, income < ₹8 lakh, AWW/AWH/ASHA. Excluded: regular government/PSU employees and those receiving a similar benefit. Miscarriage counts as a fresh case. Forms 1-A and 1-B. JSY is paid separately, giving an average of about ₹6,000 in total. Source: Mission Shakti guidelines, https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in/Content/assets/PDF/MissionShaktiGuidelines.pdf
- 4.27 crore beneficiaries and ₹20,150 crore paid (30 Apr 2026). Source: PIB 9 Jun 2026, PRID 2270668
- Portal, UMANG, MCP card plus eligibility proof, helpline 14408. Source: PIB backgrounder, Aug 2025, https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/aug/doc2025825619601.pdf. The portal has a "Track Application Status" link.
