How the EPF contribution works

You contribute 12% of basic+DA every month. Your employer contributes another 12%, split as 3.67% to your EPF account and 8.33% to EPS (pension) — with EPS capped at the ₹25,000 wage ceiling (raised from ₹15,000 on 17 Sep 2026), i.e. a maximum of ₹2,083/month. This calculator uses the simplified split below, so results are labelled approximate; if your basic exceeds ₹25,000, your actual employer EPF credit (12% of basic minus the fixed ₹2,083 EPS share) is higher, so your real corpus can be larger.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EPF interest rate for FY 2025-26?

The EPF interest rate for FY 2025-26 is 8.25%, recommended by the CBT in March 2026 and ratified by the Finance Ministry in June 2026. This is the third consecutive year at 8.25%.

How much goes into my EPF every month?

You contribute 12% of basic+DA. Your employer also contributes 12%, split into 3.67% to EPF and 8.33% to EPS (pension), with EPS capped at the ₹25,000 wage ceiling (raised from ₹15,000 w.e.f. 17 Sep 2026) — a maximum of ₹2,083 per month. On ₹30,000 basic, the simplified EPF credit is about ₹4,701 per month (12% + 3.67%).

What changed under the EPF Scheme 2026?

The EPF Scheme 2026, in force from 29 June 2026, simplifies withdrawals into 3 categories, raises the auto-settlement limit to ₹5 lakh, and targets claim settlement in about 3 days.

Is EPF interest tax-free?

Interest on employee contributions up to ₹2.5 lakh per year is tax-free; interest on contributions above that is taxable. Withdrawal after 5 years of continuous service is tax-free. Your own 12% contribution qualifies for 80C in the old regime.
