Why an offline expense tracker?

Most expense apps ask you to sign in, sync your transactions to a server and monetize your patterns. FinCalix takes the opposite approach: everything is computed and stored locally. That means it opens instantly, works on flights and in low-network areas, and your financial life is never part of anyone's dataset.

Everything in one app

The expense tracker sits alongside 45+ Indian calculators — EMI, SIP, income tax, FD, PPF, credit card payoff and more — so tracking and planning live in the same free app. See a heavy spending month? Jump straight into a budget or a goal SIP plan without switching apps.

How the health score works

The 0–100 financial health score summarizes your money habits: how consistently you stay within budgets, your savings rate and spending trends. It updates as you log expenses, giving you one number to improve month over month.
