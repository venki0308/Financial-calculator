How SCSS interest is calculated

SCSS pays simple interest every quarter — the principal stays intact and is returned at the end of the 5-year term.

Quarterly income = Deposit × 8.2% ÷ 4

Frequently asked questions

What is the SCSS interest rate in 2026?

The Senior Citizen Savings Scheme earns 8.2% per annum for Q3 FY 2026-27 (July–September 2026), paid out quarterly. The rate is unchanged for the 10th consecutive quarter and is among the highest government-guaranteed rates.

How much quarterly income does ₹15 lakh in SCSS give?

Quarterly interest = deposit × 8.2% ÷ 4. On ₹15,00,000 that is ₹30,750 every quarter — ₹1,23,000 per year and ₹6,15,000 over the 5-year term. The ₹30 lakh maximum pays ₹61,500 per quarter.

Who can open an SCSS account?

Anyone aged 60 or above (55+ for certain retirees under superannuation/VRS rules and 50+ for retired defence personnel, subject to conditions). Maximum total deposit is ₹30 lakh. The term is 5 years, extendable by 3 years.

Is SCSS interest taxable? What about TDS?

SCSS interest is taxable at your slab rate. The deposit qualifies for Section 80C in the old regime. TDS under Section 194A applies only if interest exceeds the ₹1 lakh annual threshold for senior citizens — you can submit Form 15H if your income is below the taxable limit.
