How SSY maturity is calculated

You deposit for the first 15 years only. The balance then keeps compounding at 8.2% for 6 more years until the account matures 21 years from opening.

Years 1–15: Balance = (Balance + Deposit) × 1.082
Years 16–21: Balance = Balance × 1.082 (no new deposits)

Frequently asked questions

What is the Sukanya Samriddhi interest rate in 2026?

SSY earns 8.2% per annum for Q3 FY 2026-27 (July–September 2026), compounded annually — the highest rate among small-savings schemes, unchanged for the 10th consecutive quarter.

How much will ₹1.5 lakh per year in SSY become?

Depositing ₹1,50,000 per year for the first 15 years at 8.2% grows to ₹71,82,119 when the account matures 21 years after opening — ₹22,50,000 invested plus ₹49,32,119 tax-free interest.

Who can open a Sukanya Samriddhi account?

A parent or legal guardian can open an SSY account for a girl child below 10 years of age, at a post office or authorised bank. A maximum of ₹1.5 lakh can be deposited per financial year.

When does an SSY account mature?

The account matures 21 years from the date of opening. Deposits are made only for the first 15 years; the balance keeps compounding at the notified rate for the remaining 6 years. Partial withdrawal for higher education is allowed after the girl turns 18.
