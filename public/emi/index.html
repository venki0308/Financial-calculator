How EMI is calculated

Banks use the reducing-balance method. Each EMI first covers the month's interest on the outstanding principal; the remainder reduces the principal.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
P = loan amount   r = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100   n = months

Frequently asked questions

Does a higher CIBIL score lower my EMI?

Yes. Banks price loans by credit tier — a 750+ score can get home loans near the advertised floor rate, while lower scores pay 1–2% more. The FinCalix app includes a free CIBIL-based rate simulator.

Is prepaying a floating-rate loan free?

Yes — RBI prohibits foreclosure/prepayment charges on floating-rate loans to individual borrowers. Fixed-rate loans may carry a charge of 2–4%.

Should I pick a longer tenure for a smaller EMI?

A longer tenure lowers the EMI but sharply raises total interest: ₹10L at 9% costs ₹5.6L interest over 10 years but ₹11.6L over 20 years. Choose the shortest tenure whose EMI fits your budget (banks cap EMIs near 50% of take-home pay).
