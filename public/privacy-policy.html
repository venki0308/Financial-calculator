Your Privacy Matters

FinCalix collects zero personal data. No backend, no database, no server-side storage. Everything stays on your device.

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

FinCalix does NOT collect any personal information whatsoever.

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address is collected
  • No financial data (salary, investments, EMI amounts) is collected
  • No account creation or login is required to use the app
  • No user profiles are created or stored

You can open the app and start calculating instantly — we never ask for any personal details.

2. Data Stored on Your Device (LocalStorage Only)

FinCalix stores the following data locally on your device only using your browser's LocalStorage:

  • Calculation inputs — Numbers you enter in calculators
  • Recently used calculators — For quick access on the home screen
  • Saved calculations — When you manually save a result
  • App preferences — Theme (dark/light), language choice

🔒 This data stays on your device permanently until you:

  • • Clear the app data from your device settings
  • • Uninstall the app
  • • Clear browser data (on web version)

We have absolutely zero access to this data. It never leaves your device and is never synced to any server.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect fully anonymized usage data to improve the app experience.

What IS Collected

  • • Which calculator was used
  • • Session duration
  • • Device type
  • • App version
  • • Country

What is NOT Collected

  • • Your calculation inputs
  • • Financial figures
  • • Personal identity
  • • Name or email
  • • Browsing history

All analytics data is fully anonymized and cannot be traced to any individual.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AnalyticsAnonymized usage statistics only. No personal data is transmitted.
Vercel (Web Hosting)Hosts the web app files. No user data is transmitted to Vercel servers.

No other third-party services, advertising networks, or tracking tools are used.

5. Your Financial Data

💰 Your financial data is 100% safe

  • • All numbers you enter (salary, EMI, tax inputs, SIP amounts, etc.) are processed entirely on your device
  • • We never see, store, or transmit your financial figures
  • • All calculations happen in your browser/app — not on any server
  • • There is no backend or API that receives your data

6. Children's Privacy

FinCalix is designed for users 18 years of age and above. The app contains financial calculators intended for adult use.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 years of age.

7. Data Security

Since FinCalix stores nothing on any server, there is no server-side data breach risk.

  • Your data security equals your device security
  • All web traffic uses HTTPS encryption
  • We recommend using a device lock screen to protect your locally stored data

8. Your Rights

AccessAll your data is visible directly in the app (Saved Calculations, History, Preferences).
DeleteClear app data from your device settings, or simply uninstall the app. All data is removed instantly.
PortabilityWe cannot delete data on your behalf because we never had it. You have full control.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of FinCalix after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
