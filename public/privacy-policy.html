Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

🔒 Your Privacy is Our Priority

FinCalix stores ALL data locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

FinCalix does NOT collect any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device using localStorage/AsyncStorage and never leaves your device.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Saved financial calculations
  • Bookmarked government schemes
  • Expense tracking data
  • Budget information
  • App preferences (language, theme)

This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns (which calculators are most popular, crash reports). This data is:

  • Completely anonymized
  • Does NOT include personal information
  • Used only to improve the app
  • Cannot be traced back to individual users

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Vercel: Hosts the app files (no user data transmitted)
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymized usage statistics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting (anonymized)

5. Government Schemes Information

When you view government schemes, we provide links to official government portals. We do NOT track which schemes you view or any application information.

6. Children's Privacy

FinCalix is designed for users 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is as secure as your device
  • No data transmission = No interception risk
  • We recommend setting a device lock screen

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible in the app
  • Export: Use "Export Data" in app settings
  • Delete: Use "Clear all data" or uninstall the app

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
