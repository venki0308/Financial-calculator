How NSC maturity is calculated

NSC interest compounds once a year at 7.7% and the entire amount is paid out at the end of the 5-year term.

Maturity = P × (1.077)⁵

Frequently asked questions

What is the NSC interest rate in 2026?

The National Savings Certificate earns 7.7% per annum for Q3 FY 2026-27 (July–September 2026), compounded annually and paid at maturity after 5 years. The rate is unchanged for the 10th consecutive quarter.

How much does ₹1 lakh in NSC become in 5 years?

₹1,00,000 invested in NSC at 7.7% grows to ₹1,44,903 at maturity after 5 years — ₹44,903 of interest, using the formula P × (1.077)^5.

Does NSC qualify for Section 80C?

Yes. NSC deposits up to ₹1.5 lakh qualify for 80C in the old regime. Interest accrued each year (except the final year) is deemed reinvested and also qualifies for 80C. Interest is taxable; there is no TDS on NSC.

Is NSC better than a 5-year bank FD?

NSC at 7.7% currently beats the 5-year Post Office Time Deposit at 7.5% and most bank tax-saver FDs. NSC is sovereign-guaranteed and has no TDS, but the money is locked for the full 5 years except in limited cases.
