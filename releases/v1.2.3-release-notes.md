## Full Changelog

### Critical Formula Fixes (Real-Money Impact)

- **Income Tax**: Added Section 87A marginal relief — prevents 0-to-61,500 tax cliff at 12L taxable income. Affects both the Income Tax Calculator and post-tax returns shown in FD/PPF/NSC calculators.
- **CAGR Calculator**: Removed loss clamp — final value below initial now correctly shows negative CAGR instead of forcing 0%. Added "Loss" quality tag.
- **STP Calculator**: Fixed cost-basis tracking — tax on later transfers was under-stated because cost basis was pinned to initial investment instead of shrinking proportionally with each transfer.
- **Credit Card Interest Engine**: Single-card payoff now applies 18% GST on finance charges (matching multi-card engine), so Detail page and Payoff Calculator show consistent month counts and interest totals.
- **Gratuity**: Tax exemption corrected from 25L to 20L per Income Tax Act Section 10(10).

### Regulatory & Rate Updates

- **GST 2.0 (Feb 2026)**: Compensation Cess removed (abolished by government). Added 3% slab (gold/precious metals) and 40% slab (luxury cars, SUVs, aerated drinks). Rate comparison updated.
- **Post Office RD**: Default rate updated from 6.75% to 6.7% (Q1 FY 2026-27).
- **All small savings rates verified**: PPF 7.1%, SSY 8.2%, NSC 7.7%, KVP 7.5%, SCSS 8.2%, POMIS 7.4%, EPF 8.25%, POTD 6.9/7.0/7.1/7.5%.
